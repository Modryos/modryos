These are the texts shown in the waitlist form, the app and our emails. The app and the website use them word for word.

1. Waitlist form on modryos.com

Field: Email address.

Required checkboxes (unticked at the start):

  • Checkbox: I am 15 or older.
  • Checkbox: I agree to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.

Optional checkbox (unticked at the start, and the form works without it):

  • Checkbox: Also send me news about Modryos (launch news and big updates). I can unsubscribe at any time.

Text under the button:

We will email you once to confirm your address. We keep your email and the time you confirmed. See the Privacy Policy. Questions: support@modryos.com.

We store the time of each tick, the address and the source (the waitlist form) as a record of consent.

2. Sign-in screen in the app

By continuing you confirm that you are 15 or older and you agree to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. We email you a 6-digit code to sign in.

3. The double opt-in email (waitlist confirmation)

Subject: Please confirm your place on the Modryos waitlist

Hi,

Someone (we hope you) asked to join the Modryos waitlist with this address. Please press the button to confirm. If it was not you, just ignore this email and we will delete the request in 7 days.

Button: Confirm my place (it carries the link)

Modryos, operated by Neryos. Questions: support@modryos.com

The email has no promotion in it. Only pressing the button confirms the waitlist entry. If you also ticked the news box, the news opt-in is confirmed at the same time and the email says so in one line: "You also asked for news about Modryos. You can unsubscribe at any time."

4. Footer of every launch-news email

You get this because you asked for news about Modryos. Sent by Modryos, operated by Neryos. Unsubscribe with one click: (link). Or reply to support@modryos.com and we will remove you. This is an advertising message, and you may withdraw your consent at any time.

Every news mail has a clear subject that says it is news about Modryos, and the unsubscribe link works without signing in. We act on it at once and in any case within 7 days.

5. What is transactional (part of the service)

These emails need no news opt-in. They are sent because you asked for the service, and they carry no promotion:

  • the 6-digit sign-in code;
  • the waitlist confirmation;
  • the invite and redeem code when you are approved;
  • the inactivity reminder (after 14 days) and the notice that a place went back to the waitlist (after 21 days);
  • the warning before we delete an account for 24 months of no use;
  • important service notices (for example a change to the terms, a security problem, or the end of the beta);
  • replies to your own messages and reports.

You cannot unsubscribe from these while you have an account, but you can delete your account and they stop.

6. Withdrawing

You can unsubscribe from launch news at any time with the link in any news mail, in the app (Settings, Account), or by writing to support@modryos.com. Withdrawing does not change your account.
