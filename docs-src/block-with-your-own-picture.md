---
title: A block with your own picture
description: Make a block and give it a picture you draw or bring in.
section: Getting started
order: 8
---

1. Press **New thing**, open **Blocks and items** and pick **Block**. Give it a **Name**.
2. Press **New thing** again, open **Mobs and models** and pick **Texture**. This is the picture, and its page is the painter: it opens by itself when you make it, and again when you click the texture in the project list. Give it a name.
3. Pick the size under **How big to start**: **16 by 16**, **32 by 32** or **64 by 64**. Minecraft's own blocks are 16 by 16, so choose that to match the game. Bigger sizes show finer detail.
4. Either draw it, or bring one in:
   - **Draw:** pick a colour, press **Fill**, then paint pixel by pixel with the painter's tools.
   - **Bring one in:** press **Import a PNG** and choose the file.
5. Press **Apply** to keep the picture.
6. Open your block. In **Texture**, choose your picture.
7. Press **Play** and place the block.

More on pictures: [Pictures and textures](pictures-and-textures.md). A texture is just a PNG.
