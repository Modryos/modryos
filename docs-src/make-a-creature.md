---
title: Make a creature
description: A simple creature that acts like one from the game, with your own name and health.
section: Getting started
order: 9
---

1. Press **New thing**, open **Mobs and models** and pick **Mob**.
2. Give it a **Name**.
3. In **Made from the game's**, write the game's creature it behaves like, for example zombie or cow. Empty makes a creature of your own.
4. Set **Health** (a player has 20) and **Temperament**: Passive, Neutral or Hostile.
5. Optional: choose **Its own picture** so it wears a new look. Make the picture as in [Pictures and textures](pictures-and-textures.md).
6. Press **Play**. A spawn egg for it is in your mod's creative tab.

More: [Mob editor](editor-creature.md).
