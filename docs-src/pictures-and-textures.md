A texture is the picture a block, item or creature wears. It is a PNG file.

## Draw one

1. **New thing > Mobs and models > Texture**.
2. Under **How big to start**, pick **16 by 16**, **32 by 32** or **64 by 64**.
3. Pick a colour and press **Fill**, then paint with the tools.
4. Press **Apply**.

## Bring one in

In the Texture editor press **Import a PNG** and choose the file. To bring in many, use **Import pictures** or **Import a folder**. To use a picture you already have, press **Choose a texture**.

## Sizes

16 by 16 matches Minecraft's blocks and items. 32 by 32 and 64 by 64 give finer detail. You can change it later with **Resize the picture**.

## A strip of frames

A picture made of squares one above the other, like a film strip. The game shows the squares one after another, so the picture moves, like water. Turn it on with the **Plays as frames** setting of the block or item.

## Replaces

The **Replaces** setting is only for taking the place of a picture the game already has, for example `minecraft:block/stone`. Leave it empty for your own.
