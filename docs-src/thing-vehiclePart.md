An item put into a vehicle by using it on it, that works there every so often: breaks the blocks ahead, harvests crops, lights the way, shoots, blows up, or what its own rules say.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Vehicle part


## What you can set

### Vehicle part

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Does | Its work in a vehicle. Anything else is said in its rules, under Every time it works in a vehicle. Choose one of: Only its rules, Breaks the blocks ahead, Harvests and plants crops, Sets torches where it is dark, Throws cargo ahead, Blows up on a crash or an activator rail, Picks up items round it, Shoots, Sets blocks from its cargo ahead. |
| Reach | How far it works, in blocks: how far ahead a drill breaks, how far round a farmer looks, how strong a blast is. From 1 to 8 blocks. |
| How often | How often it works while the vehicle runs. From 0.05 to 60 seconds. |
| Fuel a time | The vehicle's fuel each time it works. With too little, it waits. From 0 to 100000. |
| Adds cargo | Rows of nine slots it adds to the vehicle's cargo, as a chest. From 0 to 3. |
| Shoots | The projectile it shoots, by its game name: an arrow, a fireball, or a projectile of the mod's own. |
| Aims | Straight ahead of the vehicle, or at the nearest monster within eight blocks for each block of reach. Choose one of: Straight ahead, At the nearest monster. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Vehicle part.
