Something eaten.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Food


## What you can set

### Food

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Fills | How much hunger it fills, out of 20 (full): an apple 4, bread 5, a steak 8. From 0 to 20 hunger. |
| Keeps you full | Higher keeps you full longer. Apple 0.3, bread 0.6, golden carrot 1.2. From 0 to 5 times. |
| Eating gives | An effect the one who eats it has afterwards. Choose one of: Nothing extra, Speed, Slowness, Strength, Weakness, Regeneration, Poison, Glowing, Invisibility, Levitation, Fire resistance, Night vision, Jump boost. |
| For | How long the effect of eating it lasts. From 1 to 3600 seconds. |
| Effect level | How strong the effect of eating it is. From 1 to 10. |
| Chance | How often eating it gives the effect, out of a hundred. From 1 to 100 percent. |
| Can always be eaten | It can be eaten with no hunger to fill, as a golden apple can. |
| Eaten quickly | It is eaten in half the time, as dried kelp is. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Food.
