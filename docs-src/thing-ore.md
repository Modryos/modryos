A block that appears in the world in veins.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Ore


## What you can set

### Ore

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Pickaxe needed | The weakest pickaxe that gets its drop: iron ore needs stone, diamond ore iron. Choose one of: Wood, Stone, Iron, Diamond. |
| Found in | The ground its veins replace. Choose one of: Stone, Deepslate, Netherrack, End stone. |
| Blocks in a vein | About how many blocks one vein holds: iron 9, diamond 4 to 8, coal 17. From 1 to 64. |
| Veins in a chunk | How many veins the world tries to place in every sixteen by sixteen of ground. From 1 to 128. |
| Lowest height | The lowest height a vein is placed at; the world's floor is -64. From -64 to 320. |
| Highest height | The highest height a vein is placed at; sea level is 63. From -64 to 320. |
| In biomes | Where it is found: choose a group of biomes, such as the whole Overworld, or single biomes. |

Every block also has the settings on the [Block](editor-block.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Ore.
