Closed Beta Addendum

Last updated: 9 November 2026. This addendum adds to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy while you take part in the Modryos closed beta. Where they differ, this addendum governs the beta.

1. What the beta is

The beta is an early, unfinished version of Modryos. It has bugs, changes often, and parts may not work or may be removed. You use it to try it and to help us improve it.

2. Bugs and data loss

Pre-release software can crash and can damage or lose files. Keep your own backups. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for lost data (Terms section 17).

3. Your beta place

  • Invites and codes are personal. Do not share, sell or give them away.
  • Places are given in waves at our discretion. A waitlist spot is not a promise of access.
  • Access can end, for example when the beta ends, if you break the rules, or under the inactivity rule. We will tell you when we can.

4. Inactivity

If you do not open the app for 14 days, we send a reminder. After 21 days, your place returns to the waitlist so someone else can use it. Your account stays, and you can join the line again.

5. Licence token and safety switch

The app holds a short signed licence and renews it online; it keeps working offline for a few days. A safety switch lets us turn off one account or one app version, for abuse or a dangerous bug. We use it only when needed and tell you why when we can.

6. Please keep early features quiet

Your projects and mods are yours to show. We only ask that you do not publish screenshots, videos or descriptions of unreleased features or the beta's inner workings without asking us first at support@modryos.com. This is a request, not a confidentiality agreement.

7. Feedback

When you send ideas or feedback, you let us use them freely, for any purpose, without payment, credit or duty to you. This covers the feedback only, never your projects or mods.

8. Reports

Reports follow Privacy Policy section 5: off until you choose, shape only unless you add more, kept 12 months. Do not put passwords or other people's private details in a report.

9. Ending the beta

We may end the beta or move to a new version with new terms, and will tell you in advance when we reasonably can. Your projects and mods stay with you.

10. Contact
