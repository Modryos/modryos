# Account and privacy

## Sign in

You sign in with your email. Modryos sends you a 6-digit code. There is no password. You have to be 15 or older. During the beta you also need an invite code. See [Install](install.md).

## Settings > Account

Open **Settings > Account** to:

- see the place line for your beta account.
- change your email.
- turn Modryos news emails on or off.
- turn **Send crash reports** on or off.
- press **Download my data** to get everything we keep about your account in one file.
- press **Delete account** to remove your account and its data on our server. You type DELETE to confirm.

Your projects stay on your computer.

## What is sent, and what never is

Your mods stay on your computer. We never had them. Modryos sends only what the account and the app need to work, and a report only when you press Send. Crash reports are sent only if you turn them on or press Send for one report. When you press **Report a problem**, you see everything in the report first.

For the full list, read the [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html).
