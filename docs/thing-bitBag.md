# Bag of pieces

A bag that keeps the small pieces a carving tool takes away, by look, and gives them back when pieces are added.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Bag of pieces


## What you can set

### Holds small pieces

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Looks it holds | How many different looks the bag keeps at once. From 1 to 64. |
| Looks it keeps | The looks whose pieces go into this bag. Empty: pieces of any look. |
| Pieces of each look | How many pieces of one look it holds. A whole block is 4096 pieces. From 16 to 1048576. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Holds small pieces.
