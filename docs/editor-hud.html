HUD number bar editor

One number of the player shown over the game as a bar or words, like saturation above the hunger bar.

How to add it#

  • New thing > Screens and guides > HUD number bar

What you can set#

SettingWhat it does
NameWhat you call it here.
ShowsWhat it draws: one of the player's own numbers, a value of the player, or the block the player looks at with its readings, in a panel at the top center. Choose one of: Saturation, Hunger, Health, Air, Armor, Armor toughness, Absorption hearts, Held item's durability, Level, A value of the player, An area value where the player stands, What the player looks at.
ValueThe value of the player it draws, against the value's most.
Area valueThe area value it draws, where the player stands, against the value's most.
PlacedWhere it is drawn: over the game's own icons on the hunger, health, armor or air bar; a row of its own just above the drumsticks or the hearts (higher while air bubbles or armor show there, and stacked with the others there); beside a bar, outside the hotbar; or at the top. Choose one of: On the hunger bar, On the health bar, On the armor bar, On the air bar, Above the hunger bar, Above the health bar, Beside the hunger bar, Beside the health bar, Top left, Top center, Top right.
Icons over the game's rowA row of icons for one of the game's rows (hunger, armour, health, air) is drawn beside it. Turn on to draw the icons over the game's own, as a saturation overlay.
Drawn asA bar that fills, the number itself, ten icons as the game's own bars (two points an icon, half an icon for one), or a bar with its number. Choose one of: A bar, A number, Ten icons, A bar with its number, A veil over the screen, A veil over the sky.
Veil strengthHow strong the tint is when the number is at its most. From 1 to 100 percent.
Number saysHow the number reads: the number itself, the number out of its most (7/20), or a share (35%). Choose one of: The number, Out of its most, A share.
ShownWhen it is drawn: always, while low or high, for a few seconds after it changes, while an item is held, while sneaking, or under water. Choose one of: Always, While below a share, While above a share, For a while after it changes, While holding an item, While sneaking, While under water.
ShareThe share of its most it is compared with: shown while below it, or while above it. From 0 to 100 percent.
ForHow long it stays after the number changes. From 1 to 60 seconds.
HoldingThe item, or a tag of items, that shows it while held in either hand.
ColorThe color it is drawn in.
Wrap into layersA value bigger than one row starts again in the next color, as many times as it needs.
One row holdsHow much of the value fills one row. Ten icons hold 20, two for each icon. From 1 to 1000000000.
Layer colorsThe colors of the second row, the third and on, as color codes with spaces between. After the last one they start again.
Layers drawnOver the first row, or each one above the row before. Choose one of: Over the first row, Each above the last.
Hide empty iconsIcons not reached are not drawn, instead of a faint dark square.
Recolor the game's own rowThe mod draws the game's hearts or armor itself. After one full row, every further row's worth turns the row to the next color, so it never grows taller. Off keeps the game's own rows.
Row colorsThe colors of the second row's worth, the third and on, as color codes with spaces between. After the last one they start again.
IconAn item, or a picture of your own, drawn small beside it. None when empty.
Icon sizeHow big the icon is drawn, in the GUI's pixels. The usual size is 8. From 4 to 32 pixels.
Hidden in creativeHidden in creative and spectator, where the game hides its own health and hunger.
Nudge acrossMoves it right (or left, below nought) from its place, in the GUI's pixels. From -400 to 400 pixels.
Nudge downMoves it down (or up, below nought) from its place, in the GUI's pixels. From -400 to 400 pixels.

What it becomes in the game#

It is not part of the game itself: it describes another mod or a file, so it has no side to choose.

What it works with#

It points at: Value, Area value.
