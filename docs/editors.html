Editors

Each editor is where you set one kind of thing. Pick one to see its settings.

Blocks and items#

  • Area value: A number the world keeps by place, not by player or item: an aura in each chunk, an oil reservoir under it, radiation, a corruption that spreads, a pollution.
  • Banner pattern: A pattern woven on a banner in the loom, and shown on a shield.
  • Block: Something placed in the world: ore, stone, a crate.
  • Boat: A boat or a raft a player rows on water, with a chest or not.
  • Forms for every material: Makes a dust, a plate, a gear or any other form for every metal and gem the game has when it starts, other mods too.
  • Gene: A trait any thing can carry and pass to its young: a number such as Growth, or one of a few choices such as Coat colour.
  • Item: Something held in the hand or kept in a chest.
  • Material: What parts are cut from, any item the game's iron ingot among them: its color, and the numbers and abilities it gives a part.
  • Satellite: A launched thing that watches a place and reports back.
  • Space station: A built place in a dimension of its own that anyone or any vehicle docks with, orbiting a world at a height that rules move.
  • Species: A kind of bee, plant or animal: the gene values it starts with, what it looks like as a block, an item or a mob, and which two species bred give it.
  • Spell: A spell of its own: what it does as rules (when it is cast, when it reaches a mob or a block, every tick it is kept up), what it costs (mana or any value), its cooldown and charge-up, and how it reaches: on the caster, along a ray, as a missile or all around.
  • Value: A number or a list kept by an item, the player, a team or the world: Energy, XP, Kills, the foods a player has eaten.
  • Vehicle: Something to ride that is not alive, beyond the boat: a cart, a motor boat, a broom or an airship, a minecart of its own, a ride along a path.
  • World event: Something that happens to a whole dimension for a while: a blood sky, a fog bank, an aurora, a rift.

Crafting and loot#

  • Armor trim: A trim put on armor at the smithing table, in any of the game's materials.
  • Condition: A test in plain words, like half of the time, only in a biome, only at night.
  • Drop: What falls out when a block breaks or a mob dies.
  • Effect: Something that happens to a mob for a while.
  • Enchantment: A power added to a tool or weapon.
  • Extras for an item: What any item, the game's or yours, also does: composts, burns in a furnace, or what a dispenser does with it.
  • Game thing changed: One of the game's own blocks, items or mobs given new values, such as a block's hardness, an item's stack size or a mob's health, a structure the game makes no more, or a limit the game sets, such as the anvil's most cost.
  • Item change: What happens to an item after it is made: its count, its wear, a name, a lore line, a random enchantment.
  • Loot changed: What the game's own blocks, mobs and chests drop, changed in place: things taken out, or one thing given in place of another.
  • Loot table: A weighted list of what can come out, rolled by a quest, a crate, a mob's or a block's drop, or a rule.
  • Machine recipe: What one machine of yours turns into something else, and what it costs.
  • Other mod's recipe: A recipe for a machine from a mod yours builds on, filled in by clicks.
  • Potion: A drink that gives an effect.
  • Recipe: Make something in a crafting table, furnace, stonecutter or smithing table.
  • Recipe change: Change a recipe the game or another mod already has.
  • Recipes taken away: Take recipes the game has out of the game.
  • Ritual: Offerings on pedestals around an altar, a catalyst on the altar, the conditions it asks, and the result it leaves on the altar.
  • Station recipe: A recipe done on a station's own screen, with cells, tanks and energy.
  • Villager trade: What a villager offers and asks for.

Machines and networks#

  • Custom kind: A matter of your own beside energy and fluid: a chemical, mana, heat, whatever your mod calls it.
  • Fluid: A liquid that flows in the world and fills a bucket, as water does.
  • When fluids meet: What happens where a fluid meets another fluid or a block: lava meeting water makes obsidian, your own fluid meeting lava makes a block or drops an item.

Mobs and models#

  • Animation: How a model moves over time.
  • Attribute: A number every living thing has, as luck and armor are.
  • Damage change: A hurt a living thing takes, changed or cancelled by who takes it, who deals it and what it is, on every game version, without code of yours inside the game's own.
  • Mob: A living thing with a model, animations and behavior.
  • Mob behaviour: A piece of your own code that joins one activity of a mob's brain, the game's or another mod's.
  • Model: The shape of a block, item or mob, made from boxes.
  • Movement: How a living thing moves, the player or a mob: its speed and its jump, and a class of yours run as it moves.
  • Painting: A picture hung on a wall.
  • Particle: Sparks, smoke, dust and the like.
  • Projectile: A thing that flies, drawn by a model: a spell, a dart, a boomerang, a homing missile.
  • Sound: A sound the game plays.
  • Texture: The pixels a block, item or mob wears.
  • The player: The game's own player: its body, and rules for its moments, such as when it eats, is hurt or joins.
  • Thing: Something in the world that is not a mob and does not fly: a spell's ward, a frame on a wall, a floating orb, a balloon on a string, a trap.
  • Villager job: A job a villager takes at its workstation, with its coat and its trades.
  • Worn slots: Slots you name on any mob, the game's own or yours: a hat on a cow, boots on its four legs, a saddle on a pig, armor on a wolf.

Logic and code#

  • Another mod: A mod this one uses or works with, such as a recipe viewer: compiled against, in the test game, and named in both manifests.
  • Behavior: What happens when something happens: when this sword hits, set the target on fire.
  • Code: A Java class of your own, kept safe when the mod is rebuilt.
  • Command: A command typed in chat with a slash, whose rules run when a player types it.
  • Commands to run: A list of the game's commands run together by name, when a world loads, or every tick.
  • Damage type: A way of getting hurt, with its own message.
  • File of your own: Any file the mod ships as it is, under resources/: data, assets, anything the game reads.
  • Game rule: A rule of the world, set with the gamerule command.
  • Locks: What a player may not use, wear, break, place, hit, enter or ride until they reach a skill level, a value or an advancement.
  • Mod settings: A setting players can change after the mod is built, in its config file: a number, on or off, one word out of a list, or a list of entries they edit.
  • Tag: A named group of blocks or items other rules can point at.
  • Translation: The names of your things in another language.
  • Word of your own: A few lines you name once and use in any rule as one line: it is given as many things as it needs, and it can give back an answer.

The world#

  • Biome: A region of the world with its own look and life.
  • Caves: Caves, ravines or Nether tunnels the world cuts into its ground.
  • Dimension: A whole other world reached through a portal.
  • Feature: Something the world grows as it is made: a patch of flowers, a tree, a boulder, a lake, a vein of a block.
  • Structure: A building or ruin placed in the world.
  • World type: A choice on the game's Create New World screen.

Screens and guides#

  • Advancement: One of the game's own advancements: a goal with a toast, reached by an item, a kill or a rule.
  • Chest menu: A chest's screen of items, one to six rows of nine, opened by a rule for one player; each item runs its own lines when clicked, and nothing can be taken from it.
  • Creative tab: A tab of the creative inventory with the things you put in it, or your things added to one of the game's tabs.
  • Drawing in the world: Drawn in the world from what the player looks at: an outline, the vein a key will break, a beam from the hand.
  • Guide book: A book of pages about the mod: words, pictures, items and recipes.
  • HUD drawing in code: Your own code that draws on the HUD every frame, in a box you place on the screen.
  • HUD number bar: One number of the player shown over the game as a bar or words, like saturation above the hunger bar.
  • Key binding: A key players press to do something of yours.
  • Quests: Chapters of quests a player finishes for rewards, read in a book of the mod's own, each quest placed on its chapter's page.
  • Screen: A screen of the mod's own, laid out by dragging: opened by a rule, or shown always over the game.
  • Skill: A skill with levels, like Mining or Farming: XP comes from any moment, each level reached runs its rewards, and locks may wait for a level.
  • Skill tree: Skills a player buys with points, each after the ones it needs.
  • Tooltip lines: Lines added to the tooltip of any item or block, the mod's or the game's, or of every item, each filled in from the item's numbers and values and shown by its own condition.
