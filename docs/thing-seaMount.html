Sea mount

A swimming mob ridden under water, its rider breathing.

How to add it#

  • New thing > Mobs and models > Sea mount

What you can set#

Has a spawn egg#

SettingWhat it does
Egg colourThe colour of the egg itself.
Spot colourThe colour of the spots on the egg.

Can be tamed#

SettingWhat it does
Tamed byWhat makes it a player's: an item given to it, riding it until it calms, or only a rule's words. Choose one of: Giving it an item, Riding it until it calms, Only a rule.
Tamed withThe item a player gives it, one each try.
Chance a tryHow likely each item given, or each time it is ridden, tames it. From 1 to 100 percent.
Sits when toldIts owner's use makes it sit and stay, and stand again.
Follows ownerIt keeps near its owner when it is not sitting.
Follows fromHow far its owner may go before it comes after them. From 2 to 32 blocks.
Catches upLeft far behind, it appears beside its owner, as a tame wolf does.
Strikes back at its ownerOff, a tamed one never targets or attacks its owner or the owner's team, however it was hurt, as a wolf does not.
Defends ownerIt attacks what hurts its owner and what its owner attacks.
Healed withFoods its owner gives it to heal it.

Can be ridden#

SettingWhat it does
Flies riddenHow it flies with a rider: the jump key rises, the sprint key (Ctrl) descends, forward goes where the rider looks, A and D turn it; hovering and flapping hold their height when no key is held, gliding sinks slowly. Choose one of: Does not fly, Glides, Hovers, Flaps, Its own way.
Climbs atHow fast it rises while its rider holds jump. From 0 to 40 blocks a second.
Sinks atHow fast it sinks in the air with nothing held. 0: it hovers. From 0 to 40 blocks a second.
Dives atHow fast it goes down while its rider holds the descend key. From 0 to 40 blocks a second.
In waterFloating keeps its rider's head up; swimming dives and rises where the rider looks, and the rider breathes. Choose one of: Floats, Swims under.
Needs a saddleIt can be ridden only once a saddle is put on it.
SaddleThe item used on it as its saddle.
Ridden byOnly its owner once it is tamed, or anyone. Choose one of: Its owner, Anyone.
Speed riddenHow fast it goes with a rider, times its own speed. From 0.1 to 5 times.
Flying speedHow fast it flies with a rider, times its own flying speed. From 0.1 to 5 times.
JumpsHow high it jumps on a full charge of the jump bar. From 0 to 8 blocks.
Seat heightHow far above or below its back the rider sits; the back is found from the shape of its body. From -4 to 8 blocks.
A and DTurning turns the creature and the rider's view with it, as a horse does; strafing moves it sideways and keeps it facing. Choose one of: Turn it, Strafe, Nothing.
Mouse lookSteering, the creature faces where the rider looks, as a vanilla horse does; looking only, the view turns on its own and the creature faces where A and D turned it. Choose one of: Steers it, Looks only.
W and SForward and back move it along its body; set to nothing, only the other keys move it. Choose one of: Move it, Nothing.
Jump keyAuto rises a flier or a swimmer and makes the others jump with the jump bar; or choose to rise, to jump, or nothing. Choose one of: Auto, Rise, Jump, Nothing.
Free up and downA flier or a swimmer rises while the jump key is held, holds its height when nothing is held, and descends on the sprint key. Off, it goes up and down only where the rider looks.
Descend keyThe key held to take a flier or a swimmer down (changed in the game's Controls); the sneak key always gets the rider off. Choose one of: Sprint key (Ctrl), Swap-hands key (F), None.
Rider on a tiltFollowing, the rider leans with the seat's part of the body as it tilts (a dragon pitched in flight); upright, he stays level whatever the body does. First person stays level either way. Choose one of: Follows the seat, Stays upright.
Rider can hit itA left click while riding never lands on the creature itself unless this is on.
Falls hurtOff, neither the rider nor the creature takes fall damage while riding, and the rider none from the fall off its back.
Seat forwardHow far forward of the middle of its back the rider sits; below zero is behind. From -4 to 4 blocks.
Turn speedHow fast A and D turn it, and the rider's view with it, in degrees a second. From 10 to 360 degrees a second.
SeatsHow many ride it at once; the first steers. From 1 to 4.

Every mob also has the settings on the Mob page.

What it becomes in the game#

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Has a spawn egg, Can be tamed, Can be ridden.

What it works with#

It points at: Model, Sound, Damage type, Code.

Other things that open the same editor: Mob, Pet, Mount, Flying mount, Boss.
