# Templates and samples

A template adds a thing that already works. A sample is a small finished mod you copy and change. Your copy goes in its own folder and the sample stays as it is.

## Templates

Add one with New thing > the category shown > its name.

| Template | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Crafting station (Machines and networks) | A block with a 3x3 grid and a result, like a crafting table. |
| Powered machine (Machines and networks) | A machine with a recipe that uses energy and time. |
| Generator with fuel (Machines and networks) | A generator that burns coal into energy, ready to run. |
| Electric furnace (Machines and networks) | A machine that cooks sand into glass using energy. |
| Fluid tank (Machines and networks) | A tank that holds 8000 mB and opens its own screen. |
| Ore with a pickaxe (Blocks and items) | An ore that needs an iron pickaxe, its ingot and a pickaxe. |
| Set (Blocks and items) | Makes an ore, its ingot, tools and armor, with recipes. |

## Samples

Start screen > Samples > pick one > Make my copy.

### Ruby Gear

Ruby ore to mine, and a katana and a pickaxe made from rubies.

**What is inside:** 3 items, 1 block, 3 recipes.

**In the game, try:**

- Open the creative inventory and search Ruby.
- Mine Ruby Ore with an iron pickaxe: it drops a Ruby. Three rubies and two sticks make a Ruby Pickaxe.

### Ember Ore

A glowing ore that drops Ember Ingots, and a chest that holds more.

**What is inside:** 1 item, 2 blocks, 1 recipe.

**In the game, try:**

- Search Ember in the creative inventory.
- Break Ember Ore with a pickaxe: it drops Ember Ingots.

### Frost Magic

A frost wand, a frost blade, a chilly enchantment and a potion of frost.

**What is inside:** 3 items, 1 block, 1 potion, 1 effect, 1 enchantment, 1 painting.

**In the game, try:**

- Search Frost in the creative inventory.
- Hit a mob with the Frost Blade and watch it freeze.

### Wyrm

A flying wyrm with a boss bar that drops dragon scales when it falls.

**What is inside:** 1 item, 1 creature.

**In the game, try:**

- Take the Wyrm spawn egg from the creative inventory and use it.
- Defeat it to get Dragon scales.

### Ember Fox

A fiery fox you tame with sweet berries. It follows you around.

**What is inside:** 1 creature.

**In the game, try:**

- Use the Ember Fox spawn egg from the creative inventory.
- Feed it sweet berries until hearts show: now it follows you.

### Trail Quests

A quest book that walks you from your first log to the Nether.

**What is inside:** 1 item, 1 quest book.

**In the game, try:**

- You get the Trailhead Book when you join a world. Open it.
- Chop a log to finish the first quest.

### Hearthwood

An adventure and cooking mod: pies, a bestiary, an ember altar, a longbow and two armour sets.

**What is inside:** 4 foods and a cookbook, a bestiary book, a skill tree with 6 skills, an altar and 4 pedestals with carved models, a longbow and 2 armour sets, a magnet with a charge bar, 3 HUD gauges.

**In the game, try:**

- Craft a Golden Cookie from gold nuggets and a cookie.
- Open the Hearth Cookbook and the Wild Things book.
- Build the Ember Altar inside a ring of Stone Pedestals.

### Hidden Stats

Shows what the game hides: saturation, tool wear and armour toughness.

**What is inside:** 3 HUD meters.

**In the game, try:**

- Eat something and watch the saturation above your hunger bar.
- Hold a pickaxe to see how much wear it has left, or wear diamond armour to see its toughness.

### Garden Blocks

Brick stairs and walls, a wooden fence, gate and door, flowers and tomatoes.

**What is inside:** 11 blocks.

**In the game, try:**

- Search Garden in the creative inventory.
- Build a little garden with the fence, the gate and the bluebells.

### Carved Helm

A carved helm to wear on your head and a power chest on your back.

**What is inside:** 2 items, 1 block.

**In the game, try:**

- Search Carved Helm in the creative inventory.
- Put it on and press F5 to see it.

### Brass Works

Brass dust, ingots, rods, plates and gears, each made from the last.

**What is inside:** 5 items, 5 recipes.

**In the game, try:**

- Open a crafting table and search Brass in the recipe book.
- Turn Brass Dust into an Ingot, then into a Gear.
