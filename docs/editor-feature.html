Feature editor

Something the world grows as it is made: a patch of flowers, a tree, a boulder, a lake, a vein of a block.

How to add it#

  • New thing > The world > Feature

What you can set#

SettingWhat it does
NameWhat you call it here. The game never shows it.
Game nameThe feature's name in the game, as a command places it: /place feature.
ShapeWhat the world makes each time it places one. Choose one of: A patch of plants, A tree, A boulder, A lake, A vein under the ground, A round patch in the ground, A spring in a cave wall, A geode, A huge mushroom, A pile, A column, An iceberg, Basalt columns, A patch of ground, A fossil, A dripstone cluster, A desert well, A delta, Blobs in the ground, Kelp, Seagrass, Sea pickles, Bamboo, A mix of your features, A growth on cave walls, Nether plants, Nether vines, A huge fungus, A sculk patch, Magma under the sea, A dungeon, Vines, Glowstone, A basalt pillar, Blue ice, Coral, A chorus plant, An island in the End, One block, A replaced block, A filled layer, Frozen water and snow, An ice spike, An End spike, An End gateway, A great dripstone, A pointed dripstone, A tree with roots, Scattered ore, A ground patch under water, One of two features, A bonus chest, A patch of flowers (the game's flower type), A patch of bushes or plants (the game's random patch), One of several features, evenly, The End's obsidian platform, A starting platform in the void, Deposits from a list.
PlantsWhat the patch is made of. Each is as likely as its weight: two poppies for every dandelion. Each row has: Plant, Weight.
Plants a patchHow many times it tries to plant one in a patch. Some land where nothing grows. From 1 to 128.
SpreadHow far from the middle of the patch a plant may land. From 1 to 16 blocks.
TrunkThe block the trunk is made of: a log of the game's or a block of yours.
LeavesThe block the leaves are made of.
Tree shapeHow its leaves sit on the trunk, as the game's own trees. Choose one of: Round, as an oak, Pointed, as a spruce, Tall with branches, as a big oak, A low bush.
Trunk heightHow tall the trunk grows, before a few blocks more by chance. From 1 to 32 blocks.
Made ofThe block it is made of.
Found inThe ground the vein replaces. Choose one of: Stone, Deepslate, Netherrack, End stone.
Vein sizeAbout how many blocks a vein holds. From 1 to 64.
LowestThe lowest and highest height a vein is placed at. The world's bottom is minus sixty four. From -64 to 320.
HighestThe lowest and highest height a vein is placed at. The world's bottom is minus sixty four. From -64 to 320.
How many per chunkHow many veins it tries to place in each chunk. From 1 to 64.
Filled withWhat the lake holds, or what the spring pours. Choose one of: Water, Lava.
RimThe block around the lake where it meets the ground.
AcrossHow far the patch reaches from its middle. From 1 to 8 blocks.
Outer shellThe block of its outside, as the game's smooth basalt.
Middle shellThe block between, as the game's calcite.
InsideThe block that lines its hollow, as the game's amethyst.
CrystalsWhat grows on the inside. Left empty, nothing does.
ScatteredWhether its ores lie apart, one here and one there, as ancient debris does.
Only buriedWhether ores that would touch air are left out, as diamonds are.
Cap shapeThe red mushroom's dome or the brown one's flat cap. Choose one of: Dome, Flat.
CapThe block its cap is made of.
StemThe block its stem is made of.
Cap widthHow far the cap reaches from the stem. From 1 to 6 blocks.
Made ofThe block the pile is made of, as hay bales in a village.
Made ofThe block stacked up into a column, as sugar cane grows.
Up toHow tall it is, at most. From 1 to 32 blocks.
Made ofThe block the iceberg is made of. It floats at the sea's level in an ocean.
SpreadHow far its columns spread, as the Nether's basalt deltas. From 0 to 3 blocks.
Up toHow tall its columns of basalt rise at most. From 1 to 10 blocks.
GroundThe block laid over the ground, as moss in a lush cave.
Grows on itThe plant on most of the ground. Left empty, the ground is bare.
AcrossHow far the ground reaches from its middle. From 1 to 12 blocks.
TreeOne of your tree features. The roots grow round it and under it.
RootsThe block the roots are made of, as rooted dirt under an azalea.
EitherOne of your features. It is placed half the time.
OrAnother of your features, placed the other half. A feature here grows only through this one, not on its own.
BonesWhich of the game's own fossils it leaves underground. Choose one of: Spines, Skulls, Spines and skulls.
AcrossHow far its stalactites and stalagmites reach in a cave. From 2 to 8 blocks.
RimThe block around each pool, as magma round the Nether's lava deltas.
AcrossHow far its pools spread at most. From 1 to 16 blocks.
InThe block the blobs replace where they find it.
Made ofThe block each blob is made of, as blackstone in netherrack.
AcrossHow far a blob reaches from its middle. From 1 to 12 blocks.
TallHow much of it grows two blocks tall. From 0 to 100 percent.
How manyHow many sea pickles it tries to place. From 1 to 64.
On podzolHow often the ground under it turns to podzol, as in a bamboo jungle. From 0 to 100 percent.
Picks one ofYour other features, one of them at each place, each as likely as its weight, as a forest mixes its trees. A feature here grows only through the mix, not on its own. Each row has: Feature, Weight.
GrowthWhat grows over the stone of caves. Choose one of: Glow lichen, on walls and ceilings, Sculk veins, on every side.
ReachHow far from where it starts it looks for stone to cover. From 1 to 64 blocks.
PlantsWhat spreads over the ground, each as likely as its weight. It grows only on nylium, as in a crimson forest. Each row has: Plant, Weight.
GrowsWhich way the vines grow, as the Nether's own. Choose one of: Up from the ground, twisting, Down from the ceiling, weeping.
Grows onThe ground it grows from, as crimson nylium.
StemThe block its stem is made of, as a crimson stem.
CapThe block its cap is made of, as nether wart.
LightsThe block dotted through its cap, as shroomlight. Left empty, the cap is whole.
SpreadHow far the sculk spreads from its middle. From 1 to 32.
With a catalystHow often a sculk catalyst stands in the middle of it. From 0 to 100 percent.
Coral shapeWhich of the warm ocean's corals grows, of the game's coral colours. Choose one of: A tree of branches, A claw, A mushroom.
ListThe list of entries the player edits that says each deposit: its block, size, how rare and how deep.
Block fieldThe list's field that holds each deposit's block.
Size fieldThe list's field that holds each deposit's size, in blocks.
Rarity fieldThe list's field that holds how rare each deposit is: one chunk in this many. Missing, it is every chunk.
Lowest fieldThe list's field that holds the lowest height a deposit lies at.
Highest fieldThe list's field that holds the highest height a deposit lies at.
How oftenHow many the world places in each chunk, a square of sixteen blocks. Choose one of: Everywhere, eight a chunk, Often, three a chunk, Sometimes, one a chunk, Rarely, one in eight chunks, Very rarely, one in thirty two chunks, In clumps, thick in some places and thin in others, Just so many.
In each chunkHow many it places in a chunk where it places any. From 1 to 256.
Once inIt places any in one chunk of this many; one is every chunk. From 1 to 1000 chunks.
WhereAt what height it is placed. Choose one of: Where it belongs: on the ground, or under it, On the ground, Between two heights, Most in the middle of two heights, On the top of the world, water too, On the ground, under the sea too, On cave floors, On cave ceilings.
LowestThe lowest height it is placed at. The world's bottom is minus sixty four. From -64 to 320.
HighestThe highest height it is placed at. The sea is at sixty three. From -64 to 320.
Only on these blocksIt is placed only on top of one of these blocks. Empty means any block.
Only inKeep it to places full of water, or to open air. Choose one of: Anywhere, Only in water, Only in air.
Not near the surfaceIt stays at least this many blocks below the ground. Zero means no limit. From 0 to 128 blocks.
Only in its biomesOn, as the game does: it is placed only in the biomes above. Off lets other things that place it decide.
In biomesWhere it grows: the game's biomes, a group of them such as every overworld biome, or biomes of yours.

What it becomes in the game#

It is not part of the game itself: it describes another mod or a file, so it has no side to choose.

What it works with#

It points at: Block, Mod settings, Biome.
