# Thrown weapon

Something thrown that flies as itself and hits: a throwing star, a javelin.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Thrown weapon


## What you can set

### Shoots

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Works like | Which of the game's own it starts from: a bow drawn and let go, a crossbow loaded and then fired, or thrown as a snowball or a trident is, the thing itself flying. Choose one of: The game's Bow, The game's Crossbow, Thrown. |
| Damage | What what it shoots takes from what it hits, where a heart is 2: the game's arrow 2 before its speed counts, a trident 8. From 0 to 1000. |
| Full draw | How long it takes to draw fully; let go sooner and it shoots weaker, as the game's bow does in its 1 second. From 0.05 to 10 seconds. |
| Flies at | How fast what it shoots flies, against the game's own: 1 is the game's, 2 twice as fast and farther. From 0.1 to 5 times. |
| A hit gives | An effect every hit gives what it hits. Choose one of: Nothing extra, Speed, Slowness, Strength, Weakness, Regeneration, Poison, Glowing, Invisibility, Levitation, Fire resistance, Night vision, Jump boost. |
| For | How long the effect of a hit lasts. From 1 to 600 seconds. |
| Burning | What it shoots flies alight and sets what it hits on fire, as the Flame enchantment does. |
| Where it lands | What a thrown thing does when it hits: lies there to be picked up again, or breaks. Choose one of: Lies there, Breaks. |
| Flies | How it is drawn in flight: lying flat and spinning as a throwing star, point first as a javelin, or turned to the camera as the game draws a snowball. Choose one of: Flat, spinning, Point first, Facing the camera. |
| Shoots | What it looses: the game's arrow, or a projectile of your mod's own, which flies by its own settings. Choose one of: Arrow. |
| Shoots | What it takes from the inventory and shoots in place of arrows, yours or the game's, or any item of a tag; picked up again where it lands. With none, arrows. |
| Loading time | How long it takes to load, held before it is ready to fire; the game's crossbow takes 1.25 seconds. From 0.05 to 10 seconds. |
| Shots at once | How many it looses at once from one piece of ammunition, fanned out, as a crossbow with Multishot looses three. From 1 to 16 times. |
| Fan | How wide the shots at once fan out, in degrees from the first to the last. From 0 to 180 degrees. |
| Pause | Seconds before it can be thrown again, as the game's ender pearl waits 1. From 0 to 60 seconds. |
| Drawing | The picture as the draw begins. |
| Half drawn | The picture half way through the draw. |
| Drawn | The picture fully drawn. |
| Loaded | The picture of a crossbow loaded and ready to fire. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Shoots.
