Dimension editor

A whole other world reached through a portal.

How to add it#

  • New thing > The world > Dimension

What you can set#

SettingWhat it does
NameWhat you call it here.
Game nameThe dimension's name in the game, as a command goes there: /execute in.
Its landWhat its ground is like. Choose one of: Hills and valleys, as the overworld's, Shaped land, each biome its own, Islands floating in the sky, Caves under a ceiling of rock, Flat, in layers, Empty, nothing at all.
BiomesThe biomes it is made of, yours and the game's, each taking a share of its land.
RockThe block its hills are made of, under each biome's own ground.
SeaWhat fills its low places. Choose one of: Water, Lava, Nothing.
Sea levelHow high its sea comes. From -64 to 256.
Land heightThe height its land stands at; each biome stands above or below it by its own land height. From -32 to 256 blocks.
HillinessHow hilly the whole dimension is; each biome's roughness is a share of it. From 0 to 100 percent.
Biome sizeAbout how wide each biome's land is, in blocks. From 64 to 2048 blocks.
CavesThe caves cut into its ground: none, winding tunnels, or tunnels and great caverns as the overworld's. Choose one of: None, Tunnels, Tunnels and caverns.
Water undergroundPockets of water and lava underground at their own levels, as the overworld's; off, every cave below the sea is flooded.
Great ore veinsThe overworld's large veins of copper and iron winding deep underground.
LayersIts ground from the bottom up, each layer a block and how thick it is. Each row has: Block, Thick.
TimeWhether day turns to night there. Choose one of: Day and night, as the overworld, Always day, Always night.
SkyWhat is over it. The colours of its sky and fog are its biomes'. Choose one of: A sky with the sun and the moon, No sky, a thick haze, A dark sky, as the end's.
CloudsWhether clouds drift over it, under a sky with the sun and the moon.
Cloud heightHow high its clouds drift. Before Minecraft 1.21.11 the game keeps them at its own height. From -64 to 320 blocks.
Light in the darkHow bright its darkest places are: none is as the overworld at night. From 0 to 100 percent.
SunlightWhether light comes down from its sky.
Beds workWhether a player may sleep there. Off, a bed explodes as in the nether.
DistanceHow far one step there carries a player elsewhere: eight is the nether's. From 0.00001 to 30000000.
PortalA way in, as the nether's: a frame of a block, lit by an item. Its inside becomes a portal; standing in it takes a player there and back.
FrameThe block its frame is built of, two to twenty one wide and three to twenty one high inside.
Lit byThe item a player uses on the frame to open it.
Portal colourThe colour of the swirl inside the frame.
Settings of a made oneNumbers each dimension made from this one keeps: each starts at random between its least and its most (the same for a fixed one), and a rule may set it. Each row has: Setting, Least, Most.
Ore of a made oneA block scattered in veins through each made dimension's ground. None picked means none.
Ore veins a chunkHow many veins of the ore each chunk gets; a formula may read a setting, as ores. From 0 to 64.
Ore from heightThe lowest the veins are. From -64 to 320.
Ore to heightThe highest the veins are. From -64 to 320.
GravityHow hard everything falls in it: 100 is the game's own, 50 half, as on a moon. From 0 to 1000 percent.
Monsters' strengthMonsters in it have this much of their health and their hit: 200 is twice. From 1 to 10000 percent.
Hurt in the darkHurt a second a player takes in the dark in it; 0 for none. From 0 to 1000.
Dark at lightThe light this low or lower counts as dark: 0 is pitch black, 15 full day. From 0 to 15.
AirHow much air there is: 100 is the game's own; below it a player's breath runs out and they drown. From 0 to 100 percent.
Gives air when wornWho wears this breathes as at home: a space helmet, a mask.
Gives air fromThe fluid the worn item holds and breathes from. Empty: it gives air for ever.
Uses a secondHow many millibuckets of that fluid the worn item uses each second in this dimension. When it runs out the air goes. From 0 to 100000 millibuckets.
Start islandWhere the first one arrives in the empty, a small island is made for them.
Island topThe block on top of the island.
Island sizeHow far the island reaches from its middle. From 1 to 16 blocks.
Island heightHow high the top of the island stands. 0: as before, the arrival height (64 in the overworld). Any other number is the height you choose. From -2032 to 2031 blocks.
Island atWhere the island is built: where the world begins (spawn), or at a place you choose. Choose one of: Where the world begins, At a place I choose.
Island east-west placeThe x place of the island's middle. From -30000000 to 30000000.
Island north-south placeThe z place of the island's middle. From -30000000 to 30000000.
Holds pocket roomsWalled rooms, one for each name (a player, a placed block, an item), far apart in it.
Room wallsThe block a room's six walls are made of.
Room's middle blockA block put in the middle of a new room's floor, as a way out. None picked means none.
Rooms apartHow far apart rooms are, middle to middle. From 80 to 4096 blocks.

What it becomes in the game#

It is not part of the game itself: it describes another mod or a file, so it has no side to choose.

What it works with#

It points at: Biome, Block.

These point at it: World type.
