# Carved block

A block made of small pieces of any block's look, carved on its Carve tab or by players in the world. Its boxes can be doors, seats, ladders, lights or stores; its item keeps the shape.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Carved block


## What you can set

### Made of small pieces

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Starts as | The block whose look a new one is made of, before anyone carves it. |
| First shape | A whole block, or the shape of its own model: as pieces, or as boxes of the model's exact sizes. Choose one of: A whole block, Its model, as pieces, Its model, as boxes, The shape carved on its Carve tab. |
| Carved shape | The shape it is placed with, carved piece by piece on its Carve tab: its looks, its pieces and its boxes. |
| Pieces to a side | How fine the carving is. Fewer pieces to a side make each cut bigger. Choose one of: Sixteen, Eight, Four, Two. |
| Carves the world's blocks | A carving tool used on a plain block in the world turns it into one of these, in that block's look. |
| Looks it takes | The blocks whose look its pieces may have. Empty takes every whole, solid block. |
| Its light | The light it gives: the light of the look most of its pieces have, the brightest of them, or none. Choose one of: Most of its pieces, The brightest piece, None. |
| Rows of a store | How many rows of nine slots a box that is a store opens with. From 1 to 6. |
| Carvings to undo | How many of a player's last carvings can be undone, one at a time. From 0 to 256. |
| Ladder speed | How fast a player climbs a box that is a ladder. From 0.5 to 20 blocksPerSecond. |
| Swing time | How many seconds a door takes to open or shut. Nought makes it jump in one step. From 0 to 10 seconds. |
| Opens with a redstone signal | A redstone signal next to the block opens its door, and no signal shuts it, as a normal door does. |
| Moves blocks around it | For a door bigger than one block: the carved blocks touching it with the same door name move together as one door. |
| Door name | Blocks that touch and share this name are one door. |
| How it moves | Swing turns the whole door round its hinge. Slide pushes it along. Choose one of: Swing, Slide. |
| Slide distance | How many blocks a sliding door moves. From 1 to 16 blocks. |
| Slide direction | Which way a sliding door moves when it opens. Choose one of: Up, Down, North, South, East, West. |

Every block also has the settings on the [Block](editor-block.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Made of small pieces.
