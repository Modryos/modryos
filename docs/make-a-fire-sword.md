# Make a fire sword

Two ways. The first is the quickest.

## Way 1: the setting

1. Press **New thing**.
2. Open **Blocks and items** and pick **Weapon**.
3. Give it a **Name**, for example Flame Blade.
4. Find **Sets alight for** and type a number of seconds, for example 5. Nought sets nothing alight.
5. Press **Play**. In the game, find the sword in your mod's creative tab and hit a cow.

## Way 2: a rule

Use this when you want more than fire, such as a sound too.

1. Open your sword in the Project list on the left and click its **Behavior** tab.
2. Under **Or start with an example**, press **Set on fire** ("Whatever it hits catches fire.").
3. Or build it yourself: press **Add a moment** and pick **When (this) hits something**. Then press **Add a line** and pick **Set (who) on fire for (seconds) seconds**.
4. Change the seconds, then press **Play**.

More: [Weapon](thing-weapon.md), [Behavior](thing-behaviour.md).
