# Pointer

An item whose needle turns toward something: a transmitter on a channel, a mob you tagged, or the nearest block of a kind. It shows a map when used crouching.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Pointer


## What you can set

### Points the way

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Points to | The blocks, mobs and players on a channel; the one mob or player you use it on; or the nearest block of a kind. Choose one of: Everything on a channel, The mob or player it was used on, The nearest block of a kind. |
| Channel | The channel's name: blocks linked on it, and mobs and players a rule put on it. |
| Blocks | The blocks it looks for, or a tag of them; it turns to the nearest one within its range (Looks as far as). |
| Looks as far as | How far it looks, in blocks. For the nearest block of a kind, up to 256 and only through loaded ground; 0 looks 16 blocks. From 0 to 100000 blocks. |
| Looks again every | How often the search for the nearest block of a kind runs again. A wide range reads many blocks, so search it less often; at least every half second. From 0.5 to 600 seconds. |
| Says while held | What it says over the hotbar while you hold it. Choose one of: The name and how far, How far, Nothing. |
| Look | The game's compass with a turning needle, or your own picture that does not turn. Choose one of: A turning needle, Its own picture. |
| Map when used crouching | Used while crouching, it shows a map of the marks around you, with how far and which way. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Points the way.
