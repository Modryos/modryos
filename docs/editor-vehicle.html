Vehicle editor

Something to ride that is not alive, beyond the boat: a cart, a motor boat, a broom or an airship, a minecart of its own, a ride along a path. Steered with the game's own keys, with seats, fuel, cargo and parts.

How to add it#

  • New thing > Blocks and items > Vehicle

What you can set#

SettingWhat it does
NameWhat players see it called.
Game nameThe name the game knows it by, in small letters.
GoesHow it goes: on the ground, on water, in the air, on the game's rails, or along a path of blocks with its riders inside. Choose one of: On the ground, On water, In the air, On rails, Along a path.
Looks likeThe game's own minecart, with a block in it, or a model of its own. Choose one of: The game's minecart, Its own model.
Block in the cartA block drawn inside the cart, by its game name, as a chest or a furnace in the game's own carts. Empty shows none.
ModelThe model it is drawn by, made in the model editor. Its front is the way it goes.
AnimationA clip it plays over and over.
PictureA picture it wears in place of its model's own.
Drawn sizeHow big its model is drawn: 1 is the model's own size. From 0.0625 to 16.
WidthHow wide it is, in blocks: what can be hit and what it bumps into. From 0.2 to 8 blocks.
HeightHow tall it is, in blocks. From 0.1 to 8 blocks.
Top speedHow fast it goes at most, in blocks a second. A horse runs about 10, a boat about 8. From 0 to 60 blocksPerSecond.
Time to top speedHow many seconds it takes to get to its top speed, and to stop again with nothing pressed. From 0.05 to 30 seconds.
TurnHow fast A and D turn it, in degrees a second. From 0 to 720 degreesPerSecond.
ClimbHow fast it rises with the jump key and sinks with the sprint key, in blocks a second. From 0 to 40 blocksPerSecond.
Flies byHow it flies: hovering (a broom, a drone: the jump key rises, the sprint key sinks), on wings (a plane: its speed lifts it, it banks to turn and glides without power), or by thrust (a rocket: its engine against its weight). Choose one of: Hovering, Wings, Thrust.
Holds its heightWhen on, it stays at its height with nothing pressed; when off, it glides slowly down.
Take-off speedHow fast it must go before its wings lift it, in blocks a second. Slower in the air, it stalls and drops its nose. From 0 to 60 blocksPerSecond.
GlideWith no power, how many blocks forward it goes for each block down. From 1 to 60.
Most rollHow far A and D bank it, in degrees. The more it banks, the faster it turns. From 0 to 90 degrees.
ThrustHow hard its engine pushes up while the jump key fires it. More thrust than weight lifts it; a rule may set it from its engine blocks. From 0 to 100000.
WeightHow heavy it is against its thrust. A rule may set it from the blocks it is built of. From 0.01 to 100000.
Lands byHow it comes down: its engine brakes it near the ground, a parachute slows its fall, or nothing slows it. Choose one of: A landing burn, A parachute, Nothing.
Leaves the world atThe height at which it leaves for another world, its riders with it. 0 never leaves. From -64 to 4000 blocks.
Arrives inThe world it arrives in, by its game name: the End, the Nether, a world of the mod's own. Empty arrives over the same place in this world.
Arrives at heightHow high it arrives over the same place, in blocks; from there it comes down. From -64 to 4000 blocks.
GravityHow hard the world pulls it down, against the game's own pull: 1 is the game's, 0 floats. From 0 to 10 normal.
DragHow much of its speed it loses each second with nothing pushing it: none coasts on, all stops at once. From 0 to 1.
DivesUnder water it goes down with the sprint key and up with the jump key, and holds its depth with neither, as a submarine.
Speed on landIts share of its speed out of the water: a little for a boat, all of it for an amphibious one. From 0 to 1.
Also on waterIt floats and drives on water too, as an amphibious car.
BoostHow many times faster it goes while the sprint key is held, spending fuel as fast. 1 has no boost. From 1 to 5 normal.
Steps upHow high a step it drives up without a jump, in blocks. From 0 to 4 blocks.
SteeringHow its driver steers: by how it goes (where the driver looks in the air, A and D on the ground and water), A and D turn it, or it goes where the driver looks. Choose one of: By how it goes, A and D turn it, Where the driver looks.
Path ofThe block it follows, by its game name, or a tag after a #. It goes straight on, turns where the line turns, takes the side its rider looks to where the line splits, and stops at the end.
Drives itselfWhen on, it runs along the rails by itself while it has fuel, as the game's furnace cart; when off, only with a rider.
SeatsHow many can ride it. The first one on drives. From 1 to 8.
Riders unseenIts riders are not seen while they ride, as when carried inside a pipe.
Seat heightHow high its riders sit above its bottom, in blocks. From -2 to 6 blocks.
FuelWhat it needs to go: nothing, items put in, a fluid by the bucket, or whatever its rules give it. Empty, it stops. Choose one of: Nothing, Items put in, A fluid, What its rules give.
Fuel itemThe item it burns, by its game name, or a tag after a #. Used on it, or kept in its cargo, it fills it.
Fuel fluidThe fluid it runs on, by its game name. A bucket of it used on it fills it, and the bucket comes back empty.
Fuel it holdsThe most fuel it holds. From 1 to 1000000.
Fuel from oneHow much fuel one item or one bucket gives. From 0 to 1000000.
Fuel usedHow much fuel a second of going takes. Standing still takes none; holding its height in the air does. From 0 to 100000 perSecond.
Stops when emptyWhen on, it cannot go with no fuel left; when off, fuel only counts.
CargoRows of nine slots it carries, opened by crouching and using it. 0 carries nothing. From 0 to 6.
TankHow much fluid it carries, in millibuckets (1000 is one bucket). A block beside the rail that moves fluid by itself fills it or empties it. 0 carries none. From 0 to 1000000 millibuckets.
Tank takesThe fluids its tank takes, each by its game name (water, lava). Empty: any fluid, one at a time.
BatteryHow much energy it carries. A block beside the rail that moves energy by itself charges it or drains it. 0 carries none. From 0 to 100000000.
Machine it carriesA block of your mod with its own slots and recipes that works inside the cart while the cart moves (a furnace cart, an apiary cart). Its slots are reached by a block that moves by itself beside the cart, and they drop when the cart breaks.
Part slotsHow many parts a player can put in by using a part on it. From 0 to 8.
Built-in partsParts it always has, by their game names with commas between: a chest, a drill, a light.
Breaks afterHow much it takes from hits before it breaks and drops its item, its cargo and its parts. 0 never breaks from hits. From 0 to 1000 hearts.
Its itemThe item it drops when it breaks, and gives back when it packs up.
When leftWhat happens when its last rider gets off: it stays, or it packs up into its item in the rider's hands, as a broom. Choose one of: It stays, It packs up.
Sound while goingA sound it makes once a second while it goes, by its game name: an engine, wings, a rattle.
Sound standingA sound it makes once a second with a driver while it stands, by its game name: an engine running slowly.
Sound boostingA sound it makes once a second while it boosts, by its game name.
LitWhen it is drawn at full light, even in the dark: never, while it runs, or always. Choose one of: Never, While it runs, Always.
Hurts what it hitsHow much a mob it runs into at full speed is hurt, and it is thrown aside; slower, less. 0 hurts nothing. From 0 to 100 hearts.
How oftenHow often its While it runs moment runs. From 0.05 to 60 seconds.

What it becomes in the game#

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

What it works with#

It points at: Model, Animation, Block, Item.

These point at it: Behavior.
