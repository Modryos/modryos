# Spell scroll

An item that casts its spell once and is used up.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Spell scroll


## What you can set

### Holds spells

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| When used | What using it does: casts its spell, teaches its spells, or casts once and is used up. Choose one of: Casts, Teaches, Casts once, used up. |
| Spells | The spells listed here, or every spell of its kit the player has learned (other mods' too). Choose one of: Listed here, Learned of its kit. |
| Spells it holds | The spells it casts or teaches, in the order sneak-use goes through them. Each row has: Spell. |
| Kit | The kit whose learned spells it casts; empty is this mod's own. |
| Cost share | What share of a spell's cost it takes, in percent: 50 casts at half the mana. From 0 to 1000 percent. |
| Cooldown share | What share of a spell's cooldown it waits, in percent. From 0 to 1000 percent. |
| Power share | How strong it makes its spells, in percent: their moments read it as power (1 at a hundred), so a hurt of 4 times power hurts 6 from a wand of 150. From 0 to 1000 percent. |
| Range share | How far it makes its spells reach, in percent of their range. From 0 to 1000 percent. |
| Up to tier | The strongest tier of spell it casts. From 1 to 10. |
| Switch with | How the player chooses its spell: sneak and use, or only by rules (a key of the mod's). Choose one of: Sneak and use, Rules only. |
| Spell slots | How many spells it keeps, one a slot (a staff of several). Its listed spells fill them first; the Make a spell word puts a spell made of parts in one. Nought keeps no slots. From 0 to 9. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Holds spells.
