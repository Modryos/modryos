# Waypoint compass

An item a player marks places of their own with, lists them and travels to them, each shown on a bar along the top of the screen.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Waypoint compass


## What you can set

### Marks waypoints

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| On screen | When the bar along the top of the screen shows the waypoints: always, only while the player holds this item, or never. Choose one of: Always, While held, Never. |
| Travel from the list | Whether the list lets the player go to a waypoint at once, and what it costs. A player in creative travels for nothing. Choose one of: No, Free, For levels. |
| Levels a trip | How many experience levels each trip takes. A player with fewer stays where they are. From 1 to 100. |
| Most waypoints | How many waypoints of their own a player keeps. One more is refused until they forget one. From 1 to 100. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Marks waypoints.
