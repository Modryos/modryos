# Carving tool

A chisel or any tool that carves small pieces out of blocks or adds them, in one piece or a shape: a line, a flat square, a cube, a ball and more.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Carving tool


## What you can set

### Carves small pieces

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| It | What a use does to the block it is used on. Choose one of: Takes pieces away, Adds pieces, Swaps one look for another, Paints pieces, Places a box, Gives boxes a behaviour, Turns blocks into one. |
| Carving shape | The shape of what one use carves. Used in the air, the tool goes on to the next shape. Choose one of: One piece, A line, A flat square, A cube, A ball, A stretched ball, A cone, A tube, A wedge, A pyramid. |
| Carving size | How many pieces across the shape is when the tool is made. Crouching in the air changes it. From 1 to 16. |
| Largest size | The largest size a player can change it to. From 1 to 16. |
| Shape changes in the game | A use in the air goes on to the next shape. Off, the tool keeps the one shape it was made with. |
| Look it puts | The look of the pieces or boxes it adds. Empty: the look the player picks by crouching on a block. |
| Shapes it goes through | The shapes a use in the air goes through, in order. None chosen is every shape. Choose one of: One piece, A line, A flat square, A cube, A ball, A stretched ball, A cone, A tube, A wedge, A pyramid. |
| Looks it may put | The blocks whose look it may add, swap or paint. Empty: any look. |
| Blocks it carves | The plain blocks of the world it may carve into small pieces. Empty: every block a carved block takes. |
| Costs pieces | In survival, pieces taken away go into a bag of pieces and pieces added come out of one. In creative nothing is spent. |
| Box width | How wide a placed box is, in pixels of a block. A pixel is a sixteenth of a block, and a quarter of one is allowed. From 0.25 to 16. |
| Box height | How tall a placed box is, in pixels of a block. From 0.25 to 16. |
| Box depth | How deep a placed box is, in pixels of a block. From 0.25 to 16. |
| Box steps | Where a box may go: its corner lands on a step of this many pixels. From 0.0625 to 16. |
| Behaviour | What a box does: a seat to sit on, a ladder to climb, a light, a store that opens, or a door that opens and closes. Choose one of: Nothing, A seat, A ladder, A light, A store, A door. |
| Blocks to a side | How many placed blocks across it turns into one carved block, starting at the block used on. Choose one of: Two, Four, Eight, Sixteen. |

### Wears out

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Uses before it breaks | How many blocks it mines, hits it lands or hits it takes before it breaks: iron 250, diamond 1561. From 1 to 100000. |
| Mended with | What mends it in an anvil, such as an iron ingot, or one of this mod's own things. |
| Comes enchanted with | Enchantments it has from the first time a player holds it, the game's own or your own, each at its level. Each row has: Enchantment, Level. |
| Enchants how well | How good the enchantments it is offered are: iron 14, diamond 10, gold 22. From 0 to 50. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Carves small pieces, Wears out.
