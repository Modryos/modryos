# Generator with fuel

A generator that burns coal into energy, ready to run.

## How to add it

- New thing > Machines and networks > Generator with fuel

This makes several things at once: [Generator](thing-generator.md), [Machine recipe](thing-machineRecipe.md).

## What you can set

You set each part in its own editor, linked above.

## What it becomes in the game

Each part becomes what its own page says.

## What it works with

It does not point at other things.
