# Crop

A plant that grows stage by stage on farmland from its own seeds, as wheat, and gives what it grows when it is ripe.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Crop


## What you can set

### Grows as a plant

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Stages | How many times it grows before it is ripe: wheat grows 7 times, beetroot 3. 0 is a flower that never grows. Each stage wears its own picture; ripe, it wears the block's own. From 0 to 7. |
| Picture just planted | What it wears at this stage; with none it wears the block's own picture. |
| Picture at stage 1 | What it wears at this stage; with none it wears the block's own picture. |
| Picture at stage 2 | What it wears at this stage; with none it wears the block's own picture. |
| Picture at stage 3 | What it wears at this stage; with none it wears the block's own picture. |
| Picture at stage 4 | What it wears at this stage; with none it wears the block's own picture. |
| Picture at stage 5 | What it wears at this stage; with none it wears the block's own picture. |
| Picture at stage 6 | What it wears at this stage; with none it wears the block's own picture. |
| Gives when ripe | What breaking it ripe gives, the game's or your own, as wheat gives wheat; its seeds come too. Broken before, it gives back one seed. |
| Up to | At most this many of it, at least one. From 1 to 16. |
| Stands on | What it can be planted on and stays on: farmland as wheat, grass and dirt as a flower, sand as a cactus, or the top of anything solid. Take that away and it breaks. Choose one of: Farmland, Grass and dirt, Sand, Anything solid. |
| Drawn as | A crop is four upright pictures in a square, as wheat; a cross is two pictures crossing, as a flower or a sapling. Choose one of: A crop, A cross. |
| Name of its seeds | What the seeds that plant it are called in the game, as Wheat Seeds; with none, its own name and Seeds. |
| Picture of its seeds | The seeds in the hand and the inventory; with none, its first picture. |
| Bone meal grows it | Bone meal on it makes it grow a stage or more at once, as on wheat. |

Every block also has the settings on the [Block](editor-block.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Grows as a plant.
