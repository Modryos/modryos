Machine recipe editor

What one machine of yours turns into something else, and what it costs.

How to add it#

  • New thing > Crafting and loot > Machine recipe

What you can set#

SettingWhat it does
NameWhat you call this recipe. Leave it empty and the card names itself from what goes in and what comes out.
Game nameThe name the game files use for this recipe.
MachineThe block that runs it. Recipes name their machine, so adding one changes no block.
What goes inThe thing the machine takes from the slots it takes from.
How manyHow many of it one operation uses. From 1 to 64.
What comes outLeave this empty for a generator: it burns what goes in to nothing.
How manyHow many come out of one operation. From 0 to 64.
Second thing inA second thing that goes in with the first, as two metals make an alloy. Leave it empty for a machine that takes one thing.
How many of the secondHow many of the second thing one operation uses. From 1 to 64.
EnergyWhether the machine spends energy on this or makes it. Choose one of: Uses energy, Makes energy.
How much energyOver the whole operation, not each tick. It is spread evenly across the seconds below. From 0 to 1000000000.
Energy each tickUse this much energy every tick instead of the whole amount above. 0 keeps the whole amount. The recipe waits when the machine runs short. From 0 to 1000000.
SecondsHow long one operation takes. The game reads it while it runs, so it can come from somewhere else. From 1 to 3600 seconds.
Fluid inA fluid the machine uses up each time, chosen from the list. The machine holds it in its own tank: attach Fluid to it.
Fluid usedHow much of the fluid each time; 1000 mB is one bucket. From 1 to 1000000 millibuckets.
Fluid each tickUse this much of the fluid every tick instead of all of it at the end. 0 keeps the whole amount. The recipe waits when the tank runs short. From 0 to 100000 millibuckets.
From tankWhich of the machine's tanks: 1 is its own, 2 and on the tanks added to it. From 1 to 5.
Uses a thing of your ownMatter is a thing of your own that is not an item or a fluid, like heat or mana. The machine uses this much of it each time, from what it holds: attach Matter to the machine.
TypeOne type of the kind, by name. Left empty, the kind itself, or any of its types going in.
Matter usedHow much of the matter each time. From 1 to 1000000000.
Fluid outA fluid the machine makes each time, chosen from the list, into its own tank; it waits while there is no room.
Fluid madeHow much of the fluid each time; 1000 mB is one bucket. From 1 to 1000000 millibuckets.
Into tankWhich of the machine's tanks: 1 is its own, 2 and on the tanks added to it. From 1 to 5.
Makes a thing of your ownA matter of your own the machine makes each time, into what it holds; it waits while there is no room.
TypeOne type of the kind, by name. Left empty, the kind itself, or any of its types going in.
Matter madeHow much of the matter each time. From 1 to 1000000000.
Makes a second thingA second stock the machine makes each time, into a tank that takes it: water into oxygen and hydrogen.
TypeOne type of the kind, by name. Left empty, the kind itself, or any of its types going in.
Matter madeHow much of the matter each time. From 1 to 1000000000.
Sometimes alsoA second thing it makes now and then beside the result, as a crusher gives a little gravel with the dust. It goes where the result goes, when there is room.
How many of itHow many of the second thing each time it comes. From 1 to 64.
Genes the result getsWhat the result carries of the genes of what went in: an analyser copies and reads them, a breeder crosses two. Choose one of: None, A copy of the first's, A copy of the first's, read, The first and the second crossed, A copy of the second's, The first's, with the second's written over, One gene of the first's, at random.
First must showA gene value the first thing must show to be taken, such as a species; empty takes any.
Of the geneThe gene that value is of; none is its species.
Make one for everyWrites this recipe once for everything in the set you pick: dye colours, wood types, stone types, metals, gems, or a list of your own. Where the recipe says (each) (or <colour>, <wood>, <stone>), each one's name goes; one the game lacks is left out.
Worked out from the inputsWhat the result works out from the things the machine takes in, each by a formula: a value it holds, how many it makes, an effect and its strength, or which thing it is. The formulas see the machine too: energy, fluid and tier. Each row has: Works out, Which, Formula, Seconds.
How oftenOut of every hundred times, how many give the second thing. From 1 to 100 percent.
More in and outAny number of things more that go in or come out, beside the ones above: items, fluids or stocks of your own, each with how much and, coming out, how often. Each row has: Goes, What, Item, Fluid, Stock, Type, How much, How often, Tank.
Works only whenThe recipe only works when this holds: at night, in a biome, in some weather, after an advancement, when a setting is on, or what is around the place it is made. Every row must hold. Each row has: When, Block, Value, Dimension, Weather, Name, Setting, At least, At most, Hours, At least, At most, Blocks away, Turned round.
Only while a number > 0For advanced rules: a number worked out where the recipe is made. The recipe works only while the number is above 0. Make it a formula to use any piece of the rules.

What it becomes in the game#

It is written into the mod when you Cook or press Play.

What it works with#

It points at: Block, Custom kind, Gene, Area value, Mod settings.
