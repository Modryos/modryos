# Part

A part a tool is assembled from, cut from any material in the stonecutter, wearing its material's color.

## How to add it

- New thing > Blocks and items > Part


## What you can set

### Part

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Role | What it gives the tool: a head the material's uses, speed, damage and strength; a handle multiplies them; a binding adds a quarter of the material's uses. For armour: plates give the guard and the wear, trim gives toughness, a core gives knockback resistance. Choose one of: Head, Handle, Binding, Plates, Trim, Core, Body, Arm, Leg. |
| Made of | One row for each material this part is made of, such as an iron and leather plate. Leave it empty for a part of one material. Each row has: Share. |
| Gives the tool | Stat by stat, what this part gives the tool from its material, beside what its role gives. A row for a stat its role gives takes its place; a share of 0 takes it away. Each row has: Stat, How, From the material's, Share. |
| Jobs it gives a creature | On a creature built of this part, these jobs join its own work, as a carrying arm makes it pick things up and bring them home. Each row has: Does, What, With item, Where, Reach, While. |
| Its piece on a creature | The group of the creature's model drawn while this part is in it, in its material's color. Empty means the group named as the part; groups no part names are always drawn. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: Part.
