Projectile

A thing that flies, drawn by a model: a spell, a dart, a boomerang, a homing missile. Shot by the shoot words and a creature's Throw move.

How to add it#

  • New thing > Mobs and models > Projectile

What you can set#

The main settings are below. The Projectile editor lists every setting.

SettingWhat it does
NameWhat players see it called.
Game nameIts id in the game, small letters and underscores; the shoot words fire it by this.
ModelThe model it is drawn by, made in the model editor. Its front faces where it flies.
SpeedHow fast it leaves, in blocks a second; a snowball flies 30, an arrow from a full bow 60. It flies at this speed whatever fires it; 0 is set down where it is made, as a lit charge. From 0 to 200 blocksPerSecond.
GravityHow fast it falls: 1 falls as a snowball does, 0 flies straight as a fireball, below 0 floats up as a balloon. From -10 to 10 normal.
DamageHow much it hurts a mob it hits, in hearts. From 0 to 1000 hearts.
Flight animationAn animation of its model it plays over and over as it flies: wings beating, a blade turning.
ScaleHow big its model is drawn; 1 is the model's own size. From 0.0625 to 16.
SpinTurns a second about the way it flies, as a shuriken or a drill spins; 0 flies straight. From 0 to 20 perSecond.
DragHow much of its speed the air takes each second; a snowball loses 18. From 0 to 99 percent.
LifetimeHow long it flies before it ends where it is, or turns back when it returns. From 0.5 to 120 seconds.
KnockbackHow hard it pushes a mob it hits; 1 is a sword's own push. From 0 to 10.
Sets alightHow long a mob it hits burns; 0 sets nothing alight. From 0 to 60 seconds.
PiercesHow many mobs it flies through before it stops at one. From 0 to 64 times.
BouncesHow many times it bounces off a wall before it ends there. From 0 to 64 times.
When it endsWhat it does where it stops: vanish, stick in the block it hit as a thrown knife does, explode, break the block, or set fire. Choose one of: Vanishes, Sticks where it lands, Explodes, Breaks the block, Sets fire.
ExplosionHow big the explosion is; TNT is 4. From 0.5 to 10.
ReturnsFlies back to whoever threw it after it hits or reaches its reach, as a boomerang or a yoyo, through anything on the way.
ReachHow far it flies out before it turns back. From 1 to 128 blocks.
HomingHow hard it turns toward its target each tick: what its thrower fights, or the nearest mob. 0 flies where it was aimed. From 0 to 100 percent.
Homing reachHow far it looks for a target. From 1 to 64 blocks.
TrailThe particles it leaves behind it as it flies. Choose one of: None, Flame, Smoke, Heart, Crit, Portal, Happy, Soul, Snow, Enchanting letters, Music notes, Angry clouds, White clouds, Bubbles, A witch's sparkle, A totem's burst, A sword's sweep, End rod sparks, Glow squid light, Wax flecks, Electric sparks, Sculk souls, Water drops.

What it becomes in the game#

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

What it works with#

It points at: Model, Animation.
