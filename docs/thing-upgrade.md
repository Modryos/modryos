# Upgrade

An item a player uses on a placed block that takes upgrades: it goes in and makes the block faster, bigger, quicker to move things or cheaper to run.

## How to add it

- New thing > Machines and networks > Upgrade


## What you can set

### An upgrade

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| It adds to | Speed: the block works faster. Capacity: it holds more of everything. Rate: things move in and out of it faster. Efficiency: it uses less energy for the same work. Range: it reaches farther (a channel's range, an altar's reach, what its rules read). Energy: it uses more energy for the same work, or less with a share below nothing. Choose one of: Speed, Capacity, Rate, Efficiency, Range, Energy use, Nothing: only what it gives. |
| By | How much each one adds: 50 makes one upgrade half as much again, two of them twice. From 1 to 1000 percent. |
| Tier | Its tier, 1 to 5: it adds its share once for each tier, and goes only into a block that takes its tier. From 1 to 5. |
| Goes into | The blocks that take it, picked from the list. None picked: every block that takes upgrades. |
| It also changes | More numbers the same upgrade changes, each by its own share for each of its tiers: a speed upgrade that uses 30 more energy, a range upgrade that slows by 20. Each row has: It adds to, By. |
| It gives | A plain amount it gives the block, not a share: a signal while something rides a belt, items let down into what is under it, the blocks things are thrown up or moved ahead at its end. Each upgrade put on gives it for each of its tiers. Each row has: What it gives, Amount. |

Every item also has the settings on the [Item](editor-item.md) page.

## What it becomes in the game

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

It comes with these abilities already on: An upgrade.
