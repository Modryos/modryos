Mob editor

A living thing with a model, animations and behavior.

How to add it#

  • New thing > Mobs and models > Mob
  • New thing > Mobs and models > Pet
  • New thing > Mobs and models > Mount
  • New thing > Mobs and models > Flying mount
  • New thing > Mobs and models > Sea mount
  • New thing > Mobs and models > Boss

What you can set#

SettingWhat it does
NameWhat players read above it and on its spawn egg, in any words: spaces and capitals are fine, like "Ruby Blade of Sam". The Game name is the short id the game uses instead.
Game nameThe id the game and its commands use: small letters, digits and underscores, like katana. In commands and recipes the mod's id and a colon go in front: for a mod called rubygear, katana is written rubygear, a colon, then katana. Players never read it; that is the Name. /summon uses it. Change it later and it is renamed everywhere.
BodyThe model it wears: one made for a mob, with its feet on the ground and its front facing away from you.
HealthHow much damage it takes to kill it. A player has 20, which is ten hearts.
TemperamentPassive runs when hurt. Neutral fights back when hurt. Hostile goes after the nearest player. Choose one of: Passive, Neutral, Hostile.
Gets about byHow it gets about: walking, climbing walls, flying, fluttering with no aim, swooping (circling and diving), swimming, swimming and leaping out, or set in a wall face. Choose one of: Walking, Climbing, Flying, Fluttering, Swooping, Swimming, Swimming and leaping, Set in a wall, Walking and flying.
Circles atHow high it circles over where it is, or over what it fights, before it dives. From 1 to 64 blocks.
Circle sizeHow wide the circle it flies round is, in blocks from its middle. From 1 to 64 blocks.
Leaps everyHow often it jumps out of the water ahead of it, when swimming near the top. From 1 to 600 seconds.
Takes off aboveIt walks, and lifts off when what it hunts is this many blocks above it, or when it falls. A rule can also make it take off. From 0.5 to 64 blocks.
Lands withinIt flies down and lands when what it hunts stands on the ground this close. From 1 to 64 blocks.
Lands afterHow long it stays up with nothing to hunt before it comes down. 0 means it stays up until a rule lands it. From 0 to 600 seconds.
Sunk into the wallHow deep its back sits in the block of the nearest wall face it holds to. From 0 to 4 blocks.
Settles onThe blocks it now and then lands on and rests a while: flowers for a butterfly. Empty: it never settles.
Rests forHow long it sits on a block before it flutters off again. A player coming near startles it sooner. From 0 to 600 seconds.
SizeHow big it is drawn and how big it stands, its hitbox with it: 1 is the size of its body, 0.5 half, 2 twice. From 0.0625 to 16.
Its own pictureA picture in place of the one its body wears, laid out the same way: a variant wears the same body in other colours.
Other looksOther pictures it can wear, laid out as its own is. A rule puts one on (Make it wear the look ...), and it keeps it when the world is saved. Each row has: Called, Picture.
Its own numbersNumbers each mob keeps, such as its anger. Rules set and read them like any value. Each row has: Name, Most.
WidthHow wide it is to hit and to collide with, in blocks.
HeightHow tall it is to hit and to collide with, in blocks.
Made from the game'sThe game's own thing this one behaves like; its behaviour stays the game's. A creature from the game, such as a zombie or a cow, written with its game name. Empty: a creature of its own.
Counted asWhich of the game's own groups it belongs to, which decides how many of it the world keeps around and when they spawn. Choose one of: An animal, A monster, Ambient, like a bat, A water animal.
SpeedHow fast it walks. A cow walks at 0.2 and a zombie at 0.23.
Attack damageHow much one of its hits hurts, before armour.
ArmourHow much of each hit its armour takes away, as a player's armour bar counts it.
Armour toughnessHow well its armour holds against strong hits.
Knockback resistanceHow much of a hit's push it ignores: 0 is none of it, 1 is all of it.
Follow rangeHow far away, in blocks, it notices a target and keeps after it.
Flying speedHow fast it flies, for a mob that flies.
Attack knockbackHow far its hits push what they land on.
Step heightHow high a step it walks up without jumping, in blocks.
Sound it makesA sound, the game's or your own, it makes now and then. Empty keeps the game's.
Sound when hurtA sound, the game's or your own, it makes when it is hurt. Empty keeps the game's.
Sound when it diesA sound, the game's or your own, it makes when it dies. Empty keeps the game's.
Boss barShows its health as a bar across the top of the screen to every player near it, as the Wither does.
Takes no hurt fromThe damage types it shrugs off, the game's own and your own: a blaze takes no fire, a snow golem no freezing.
Tips over when it diesWhether it falls on its side when it dies, as the game's own animals do. Off, it stays standing while it fades.
What it is doingWhich animation it plays for what it is doing. Edited on the Animations tab.
Order of its behavioursWhich of its behaviours comes first. Set by dragging them on the Mind tab, or with Move up and Move down.
Thinks every tickA piece of your own code that the game runs twenty times a second for every one of these mobs, after its own goals, for what the settings here cannot say: a flight that circles and dives, a head that watches its own target.
Poses every frameA piece of your own code that turns, moves, grows or hides its parts every frame it is drawn, after its animations: a body per stage of growth, segments that follow the head.

What it becomes in the game#

It is added to the game, so a player needs your mod to see it.

What it works with#

It points at: Model, Sound, Damage type, Code.

These point at it: World event, Drop, Behavior, Biome, Structure, Game thing changed, Species, Worn slots.
