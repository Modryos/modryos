Data Deletion

Draft for legal review. This text is not final.

Last updated 28 October 2026

For you: how to delete your data

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Modryos and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account and its data on our server at once. That includes your sessions, your reports and your waitlist entries. Projects on your own computer are not touched. They are yours, so delete them yourself if you want.

By email

  1. Write to support@modryos.com from the email address of your account.
  2. Say that you want your account and data deleted. You can also ask for a copy of your data first.
  3. We confirm the request from that address, then delete within 30 days, normally within 7. We write to you when it is done.

If you cannot write from that address, tell us what you can. We may ask a few questions so we are sure the data is yours.

Only the launch news, or only the waitlist

Use the unsubscribe link in any news mail, or write to support@modryos.com and ask to be removed from the waitlist.

What happens to backups

We keep daily backups for up to 30 days. Your data leaves them as they roll over, so after at most 30 days it is gone from everywhere. If a backup is ever restored, we delete your data again before it is used.

What we may keep

Only a hashed record that a deletion happened (no email in plain text), for as long as we need to prove we did it, and anything the law requires us to keep.
