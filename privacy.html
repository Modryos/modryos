This policy explains what personal data Modryos collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We tried to write it in plain words. If something is unclear, write to us at support@modryos.com.

1. Who we are

Modryos is a free Windows desktop studio for making Minecraft mods. It is operated by Neryos (an independent developer). In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Modryos, operated by Neryos. We decide why and how your data is used, so we are the "controller" under data protection law.

Modryos is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.

You can reach us, including for any privacy request, at support@modryos.com.

2. What stays on your computer

The projects you make in Modryos stay on your own computer. We do not upload them and we cannot see them. The app also keeps its own settings and your sign-in session on your computer. The mods you make are yours (exports carry the MIT licence by default).

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no selling of your data.
  • No tracking cookies.
  • No analytics in the app.
  • No automated decisions that have a legal effect on you. The kill switch is used by a person, for abuse or a dangerous bug.

5. Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): to run your account and the beta.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): security, rate limiting, abuse prevention, fixing bugs and backups. We weighed these against your rights. You can object (see section 9).
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): launch-news emails and the waitlist confirmation. You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing does not change what we did before.

Sign-in codes, the waitlist confirmation, invites, inactivity reminders and important service notices are part of the service, not marketing (see the Email Consent text).

6. Who receives your data

We use a few providers (processors) who handle data only for us and under their own data processing terms:

  • OVHcloud: hosts our server, a VPS in London, United Kingdom.
  • Resend (Resend, Inc., USA): delivers our emails. We send from its EU region.
  • ImprovMX: forwards mail sent to support@modryos.com to the operator's own inbox.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases): hosts the website and the downloads. GitHub sees visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy policy.
  • An external uptime monitor checks that our server is up. It sees only our server and no personal data.

We may also disclose data if a law or a court order requires it, or to protect people from serious harm. We do not give your data to anyone else.

7. Where your data goes

Our server is in the United Kingdom, which has an EU adequacy decision (the EU has accepted that its data protection is good enough). Resend is in the USA. For that transfer we rely on Resend's data processing terms, standard contractual clauses and, where it applies, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Ask us if you want details.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account, or until 24 months of no use. Before we delete for no use, we email you a warning.
  • Reports: 12 months.
  • Waitlist entries: until you get access or ask to be removed. Entries you never confirm are deleted after 7 days.
  • Launch-news list: until you unsubscribe.
  • Access logs with IP addresses: about 30 days, rolling.
  • Backups: kept up to 30 days. A deleted account leaves the backups as they roll over.
  • Sign-in codes: they expire in 10 minutes.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you your data (access);
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it in some way, or object to a use based on legitimate interests;
  • give you a copy you can move elsewhere (portability: we export it on request);
  • withdraw your consent whenever you like.

Write to support@modryos.com. We answer within 30 days. We may ask you to write from the account's email address so we know it is you.

You can delete your account yourself in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. See our Data Deletion text.

You can complain to a data protection authority. That can be the privacy authority of the country where the operator is based, or the authority of the country where you live (in the EU, your national data protection authority; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). We would like the chance to fix things first, but you do not have to ask us first.

10. Notices some privacy laws require

Some privacy laws, including the law of the country where the operator is based, ask us to tell you: giving us the data is voluntary and not a legal duty; we use it for the purposes in section 3; and it goes to the recipients in section 6.

11. Security

  • TLS (encrypted connections) everywhere.
  • Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Rate limits against abuse.
  • Least access: only the operator can reach the server and the data.
  • Daily backups are kept on the operator's own computer. We do not promise that the disk is encrypted.
  • If a breach puts you at risk, we will tell the people affected and the authorities as the law requires.

No system is perfectly safe, but we work to keep yours safe.

12. Children

Modryos is for people aged 15 and over. We ask your age when you sign up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it. If you are under 18, please read these texts with a parent or guardian. Where the law requires it, we may ask for a guardian's agreement.

13. Cookies

The website sets no cookies and no tracking. See the Cookie Note.

14. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will email account holders or show a notice in the app before it starts. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
