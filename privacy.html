Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. Version 1.0.

1. Who we are

Modryos is operated by Neryos, an independent developer in Israel ("we", "us"). We decide why and how the personal data described here is used, so we are its controller. Contact for any privacy request: support@modryos.com.

2. The short version

  • Your projects stay on your computer. We do not upload or see them.
  • We hold only what the beta needs: your email, a yes/no age check, your beta place, and reports you choose to send.
  • Crash reports are off until you turn them on. Project content is never sent unless you tick it.
  • The AI chat talks to your own AI provider directly. Your key and your chats never reach us.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, no analytics in the app, no selling of data.

3. What stays on your computer

Your projects, their history, the record of which parts the AI made, your settings, your AI keys (encrypted with your operating system's protected storage) and your chat history stay on your computer. We cannot access them.

5. Crash reports

When the game crashes or a build fails, the studio offers to send a report. Nothing is sent unless you press send, or you have turned on automatic reports in Settings (off by default; you can turn it off at any time). A report holds the log, the studio and Minecraft versions, the loader, your operating system and its version, and the shape of your project (how many of each kind of part, not their names or content). It holds no user name, email, account id, computer name or home folder path. Your project's content is included only if you tick "Include my project".

6. The AI chat (bring your own key)

The AI chat sends your messages, and the project parts and pictures you allow, directly from your computer to the AI provider you choose (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, another OpenAI-compatible service, or a model on your own computer). That provider processes them under its own terms and privacy policy, as your provider, not ours. We do not receive your key, your messages or the answers, and we are not responsible for the provider's handling. Before you add a key, the studio tells you what will be sent. A local model sends nothing over the internet.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no data selling, no tracking cookies, no analytics in the app.
  • No profiling, and no automated decisions with legal or similar effects on you. The safety switch is used by a person, for abuse or a dangerous bug.
  • No location tracking.

8. Who receives data

Only providers who process data for us, under their data processing terms:

  • OVHcloud: hosts our server (London, United Kingdom).
  • Resend (USA, EU sending region): delivers our emails.
  • Our email forwarding and mailbox provider: receives mail sent to support@modryos.com.
  • GitHub: hosts the website and downloads; it sees visitors' IP addresses under its own policy.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: checks that a sign-in is not a bot; it processes technical data under its own policy.

We may disclose data when a law or court order requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm. Nobody else receives your data.

9. International transfers

Our server is in the United Kingdom, which the EU recognises as providing adequate protection. Israel is also recognised by the EU as adequate. For providers in the USA we rely on their standard contractual clauses and, where it applies, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Ask us for details.

10. How long we keep data

  • Account: until you delete it, or after 24 months without use (we warn you by email first).
  • Reports: 12 months.
  • Waitlist: until you get access or ask to leave; unconfirmed entries 7 days.
  • Launch news: until you unsubscribe.
  • Server logs with IP addresses: about 30 days.
  • Backups: up to 30 days; deleted data leaves them as they roll over.
  • Sign-in codes: 10 minutes.
  • After deletion we may keep a hashed record that the deletion happened, as proof, without your email in readable form.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to see, correct, delete or export your data, to limit or object to a use based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect earlier use). Write to support@modryos.com; we answer within 30 days and may ask you to write from your account's email so we know it is you. You can delete your account yourself in Settings > Account > Delete account.

You may complain to a data protection authority: in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority; in the EU, the authority of your country; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office. We would welcome the chance to fix things first, but you do not have to ask us first.

12. Young people

Modryos is for people 15 and over. We ask for an age confirmation at sign-up and do not collect birth dates. If you are under 18, read this policy with a parent or guardian. Privacy settings start at their most private: crash reports off, no news unless ticked, no tracking. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 15, we delete it.

13. Security

Encrypted connections (TLS) everywhere; codes and session tokens stored only as hashes; rate limits and bot checks against abuse; only the operator can reach the server and the data; daily backups. No system is perfectly safe. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

14. Notices certain laws require

Under the privacy laws that apply to us, including Israel's Privacy Protection Law: providing the data is voluntary and not a legal duty; it is used for the purposes in section 4; and it is shared only with the recipients in section 8.

15. Cookies

The website sets no tracking cookies. The sign-in bot check may use strictly necessary technical data.

16. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell account holders by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
