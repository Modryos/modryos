This adds to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. It applies to you while you take part in the Modryos closed beta, run by Modryos, operated by Neryos (an independent developer). If the two disagree, this agreement wins for the beta.

1. What the beta is

The beta is an early version of Modryos. It is not finished. It has bugs, it changes, and parts of it may not work or may disappear. You use it to try it and to help us make it better.

2. Bugs and data loss

Pre-release software can crash and can damage or lose files. Keep your own backups of your projects and anything you care about. We are not responsible for data you lose, to the extent the law allows (see section 9 of the Terms of Use).

3. Your beta place

  • Invites and redeem codes are personal. Do not share, sell or give them away.
  • Beta places are given in waves, by the operator's choice. A waitlist spot is not a promise of access.
  • Your access can end, for example if the beta ends, if you break the rules, or under the inactivity rule below. We will tell you when we can.

4. The inactivity rule

If you do not start the app for 14 days, we send you a friendly reminder. After 21 days without starting it, your beta place goes back to the waitlist so someone else can use it. Your account stays. You can get back in line, and we will try to give you a place again.

5. The licence token and the kill switch

The app holds a short-lived signed licence token and renews it online. It keeps working offline for a few days. A kill switch lets us turn off one beta account or one app version, for abuse or a dangerous bug. We will use it only when needed and tell you why when we can.

6. Please keep early things quiet

You do not have to keep secret what you make with Modryos. Your projects and mods are yours. We ask only that you do not publish screenshots, videos or descriptions of unreleased features or of the beta's inner workings without asking us first at support@modryos.com. Talking about your own mods and how you made them is fine.

7. Feedback

When you send ideas, reports or other feedback, you give us the right to use it freely, for any purpose, with no payment and no credit needed, and without any duty to you. This does not give us any right to your projects or mods. It only covers the feedback itself.

8. Reports

The report button sends a message that you write, with app logs and system details, and maybe a screenshot. You see all of it before you send it. We use reports only to fix problems and keep them for 12 months (see the Privacy Policy). Please do not put passwords or private details of other people in a report.

9. Ending the beta

We may end the beta, or move to a new version with new terms. We will tell you in advance when we reasonably can. Your own projects and mods stay with you.
