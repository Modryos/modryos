The website

The Modryos website (modryos.com) sets no cookies. It has no tracking, no analytics and no advertising scripts.

The waitlist form sends only what you type into it, and the choices you tick, to our server.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps normal hosting logs, which include visitors' IP addresses, under GitHub's own privacy policy. We do not control those logs.

If this ever changes, we will tell you first and ask for your choice before anything is set.

The app

The Modryos app stores its own settings and your sign-in session on your computer. It does this because you asked for the service. It is not used to track you, and the app has no analytics.
